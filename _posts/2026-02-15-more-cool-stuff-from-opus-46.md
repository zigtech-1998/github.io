Clicked on a Claude-suggested prompt to start [a new conversation](https://claude.ai/share/493eacd3-d5da-4b7c-8e78-fdd117a80af2). First time doing this. The prompt was "explore decision making frameworks." Claude asked me two follow-up questions:
- What types of decisions do you want to optimize?
	- *Technical/product decisions, and career/professional choices*
- What is your main goal with this decision framework?
	- *Make faster decisions with less analysis paralysis*

While I worked with Claude Code in the CLI to troubleshoot a bookmark favicon issue with this blog, Claude Opus 4.6 created [this interactive decision framework analyzer .html file](https://github.com/zigtech-1998/github.io/blob/main/decision_framework.html) that helps you pick decision-making tools based on your answers to a few simple questions about the type & constraints of the decision at hand. Not sure how much I will actually use it, but feels like there is potential here. Either way, it's pretty damn cool how polished this product is given how little effort I personally had to put into making it (basically zero - I didn't even generate the idea myself). The tool is available to download from [my conversation with Claude](https://claude.ai/share/493eacd3-d5da-4b7c-8e78-fdd117a80af2) and in my [GitHub repo](https://github.com/zigtech-1998/github.io/blob/main/decision_framework.html). Give it a try if you want.
