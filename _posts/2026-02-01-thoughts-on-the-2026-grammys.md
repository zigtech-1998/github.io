1. Addison Rae is making music?
	- "Making music" might be a stretch. Seemed like she was really just TikTok dancing with some Autotuned voice tracks and Garageband instrumentals in the background
2. Very well deserved best new artist award for Olivia Dean
	- I can't claim to be a huge Olivia Dean fan. Or a fan of any *Best New Artist* nominee. That said, many of the Best New Artist performances were very disappointing (see #1 above). But, there were a couple good performances, and Dean's was fantastic.
3. Justin Bieber is still standing on business. Such a power move to show up to *THE GRAMMYS* wearing nothing but boxers and socks. And the 2 Chainz "Yeaahhh" ad-libs were hilarious
4. Really liked Bad Bunny's message about fighting hate with love. Reminiscent of MLKs timeless quote "hate cannot drive out hate. Only love can do that." As angry as everyone is right now, we'd do well to remember this.
