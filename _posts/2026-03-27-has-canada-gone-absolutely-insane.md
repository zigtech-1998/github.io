I've heard some buzzing about the increased use of 'medical assistance in dying' (MAID) in the Canadian healthcare system. Haven't really thought much about it until I stumbled across a phenomenally-titled article in [The Free Press called *Never Kill Yourself*.](https://www.thefp.com/p/never-kill-yourself) For starters, it cites that MAID accounted for 4.3% of all Canadian deaths in 2023 (over 15,000 total!!). Even worse is the chilling case study of the author's own experience. Speaking of her experience presenting to the E.R. with back pain:

>*After running through the usual questions about what was hurting and how much, she said, as casually as one might offer a cup of tea: “Do you want MAID?*
>
>*She sounded eerily like the doctor who had offered it to my husband—as if she was reading from a script.*
>
>*I said, as firmly as I could manage: “No, thank you.”*
>
>*She heard my refusal, took one look at my daughter’s and sister’s faces, and swiftly changed the subject. The polite, distinctly Canadian tone of the exchange made the situation seem all the more absurd.

I'd probably need a weeklong uninterrupted isolation retreat to unpack all the shit that's gone sideways in this particular scenario.

Does your 'healthcare' start wantonly killing people when you make it 'free'?
