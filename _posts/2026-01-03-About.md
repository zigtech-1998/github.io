Glad you've made it here, whether by miracle or accident. I should probably let you know what you are (and *aren't*) going to find here. In the interest of moving onto the more interesting piece of this project (I.e. learning crude web design/GitHub hosting), I'm throwing together this landing page quickly so I have something to post. So, please excuse this section’s dry *Is/Is Not* style - that's the best way this engineer can tackle the subject (for now). Hopefully I'll find a better way to convey this message as my writing improved, but might as well start with the bar extremely low. It's only up from here.

**Is Not:**
Contrary to typical convention, let's get the (hopefully) less interesting *Is Not* portion out of the way first. Importantly, the content in this blog is not advice. This blog is simply a collage of screenshots documenting pieces of thoughts and activities that make up my life at certain discrete timepoints. These should not be interpreted as claims or judgements on whether you should follow suit. More importantly, this blog is not a statement of immutable fact, nor is it a declaration or permanent opinion on any given subject. While the content here reflects what I was thinking at the time of publish, it is entirely possible (if not likely) that my opinions have changed significantly since the publish date of any particular post.

**Is:**
Now onto the better bit. What you will find here is a little side project designed capture some snapshots of how I'm spending my free time. I am hoping that this semi-public forum will be a forcing function to produce something consistently with at least mediocre quality. At the time of this initial landing page commit, I don't have any idea how this blog will be structured. My interests have tended to pinball rapidly between diverse and disparate subjects like sports, finance, AI, writing, reading, history, the 1960s space race, economics, medicine, fitness, etc, etc... with possible future interests in 3D printing, woodworking/DIY house projects, yoga, blogging, etc, etc.. I don't know which (if any) of these I will focus on here. All I'm currently armed with is an Obsidian Vault with a few scratch daily notes and a bunch of unreviewed book highlights. The only initial hopes I have are that this project will force me to document some aspects of my life that may be useful to reference later (porch stories for the grandchildren?) and fix my rapidly decaying attention span by requiring extended attention to one task (writing). Above all else, I hope this process will preserve my cognitive capacity and maintain a uniqueness of expression in a world where thinking, writing, and creativity that was exclusive to humans is now being outsourced to AI. While an AI could certainly generate much more interesting blog content than I, the ability to think critically and creatively about the world, and about one's position within it, certainly improves the human experience, regardless of whether silicon-based intelligence renders this process output insignificant.

If you're mildly interested in any of this personal trivia, proceed with caution. If not, feel free to check back in later after the awkward infant blog stage has passed and this page's framework is more developed (no guarantees on when/if this will happen).
