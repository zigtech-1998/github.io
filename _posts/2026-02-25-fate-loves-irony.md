Never before in history have the middle and lower classes had more access to wealth-generating tools and opportunities than now. For $20/month, AI models give anybody the power of an entire team of senior software devs and access to a PhD level education. The stock market, specifically whole market index funds/ETFs, allow anyone to be a "capitalist" and profit off the growth of the supposedly evil large corporations. Neither of these tools require massive amounts of money or a genius-level IQ to use profitably. A modest amount of elbow grease and a can-do attitude should do the trick. The only roadblock is a victimhood mentality.

The irony of our moment is that these very tools of upward mobility have become the latest target of "progressives" who are ostensibly the proletariat's champion in the eternal battle against the bourgeoise. Through taxation and regulation, the "people's party" is destroying the tools of individualism and ensuring the populace's government dependence. Ensuring dependence creates an easy path for progressives to win re-election by doubling down on bad policy. Want to be the favorite candidate of a dependent population? Just offer the most "free" stuff while taxing society's real producers to pay for it.

Before you reach for your pitchfork, I'd like to clarify that this isn't simply a vendetta against the American left. The Republican's insistence on compliance with authority and leaving your guns at home as ICE patrols Minneapolis streets is equally pitiful.
