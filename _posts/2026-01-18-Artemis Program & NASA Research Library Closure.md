*...library staff are working on a 60-day review of the collection and have been reportedly been given permission to save the most rare 10%-15% of it, with the remainder to be picked up by the [General Services Administration](https://www.reddit.com/search/?q=General+Services+Administration&cId=6e95c5c1-d872-4783-a9e8-08c0d7e4c609&iId=2bf6fe08-c6b0-443d-bc2b-db45347d865d)*.

*...the federal government will store some of the collection in warehouses, and trash the rest."*

Without reading any more than a few headlines and this Reddit post, I am pretty disgusted at this waste. Why are we simply trashing research material & artifacts from man's greatest (and ongoing) adventure?? This is just sad... one more reason this second Trump administration is turning out to be a monumental failure. 

I've also just learned that all scheduled Artemis launches after Artemis III have been canceled. The [budget proposal](https://mynews13.com/fl/orlando/space/2025/05/02/nasa-to-retire-moon-rocket-after-artemis-iii-mission) which cut these missions states that the new budget will result in a "leaner Science program that reflects a commitment to fiscal responsibility". 

A quick Wikipedia search shows that the Artemis program has cost a total of [$146 billion in taxpayer dollars since 2012](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemis_program#cite_note-NASA_OIG_2021-1). In other words, the Artemis program has consumed fewer taxpayer dollars in the last 13 years than the federal government lost to waste, fraud, and abuse between FY 2018 and 2022 (an [estimated $231-$521 billion](https://www.gao.gov/fraud-improper-payments)). 

My personal fascination with space tech & exploration is certainly introducing bias here, and I get that our country has plenty of issues to grapple with here on Earth. But surely a focus on "fiscal responsibility" should prioritize minimizing waste, fraud, and abuse before cutting funding to hard science and technology development programs. 

I've done minimal in-depth research on this, but on a whole it does not suggest our nation will be committed to space exploration. Please let me know why I am wrong about this (I hope that I am).
